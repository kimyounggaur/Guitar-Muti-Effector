# Cabinet impulse responses

The 4×12 dynamic and mixed microphone responses are unmodified WAV files from
Jester's Brutal Pack 1.0 by Jester Dyne Productions (Bastian Karschewski).
They are recordings of a modified 4×12 cabinet. The author's handbook inside
the original download states the pack is dedicated under CC0 1.0, including
permission to redistribute and adapt it without conditions.

- Source: https://www.jester-dyne-productions.com/brutal-ir-pack/
- Original download and handbook: https://www.jester-dyne-productions.com/content/files/2023/04/JestersBrutalPack_1.0.zip
- CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- `cab_4x12_dynamic.wav`: original `1_Cookie_Monster_44.wav` (SM57).
- `cab_4x12_mixed.wav`: original `4_Kaiju_Tamer_44.wav` (e606 and SM57).

Other cabinet types and microphone choices use the app's synthetic fallback.
The factory pack does not contain ribbon or condenser microphone captures;
those options must not be described as recorded microphones.
